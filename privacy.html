Privacy Policy

Your privacy,
respected.

Last updated 27 October 2026

This policy explains what personal information T.GANG collects, why, who we share it with and your rights.

1. Who we are

T.GANG is a chic and gym-wear brand based in Lagos, Nigeria. For privacy questions, contact us on WhatsApp +234 915 999 9813.

2. What we collect

  • Order and checkout details: name, phone, email, delivery address, country, the items you buy and your order reference.
  • Payment: payments are handled by Paystack or Flutterwave. We receive a payment confirmation; we never see or store your full card number or PIN.
  • Account: name, email and a securely stored password, or your Google sign-in or passkey. We never see your Google password, and passkeys stay on your device.
  • Your activity: wishlist, reviews, waitlist and back-in-stock requests, TG Rewards points, gift-card codes and balances, and gift-card recipient name and email you enter.
  • On your device: your bag, wishlist, chosen currency and sign-in session are saved in your browser’s local storage. We estimate your country to show local prices; we don’t store your exact location.

We don’t use advertising trackers or analytics cookies.

3. Why we use it

  • To process, ship and support your orders, returns and exchanges (contract).
  • To run your account, wishlist, rewards and gift cards (contract).
  • To send messages you asked for, such as restock alerts (consent — you can opt out any time).
  • To prevent fraud, keep records and meet legal duties (legal obligation and legitimate interests).

We don’t sell your information.

4. Who we share it with

  • Supabase — account and database hosting.
  • Paystack and Flutterwave — payment processing.
  • DHL Express and FedEx — shipping, including customs details for international orders.
  • Google — only if you choose Google sign-in.
  • WhatsApp (Meta) — when you choose to message or order there.
  • Email provider — we may use Resend to send account and gift-card emails.
  • GitHub Pages — website hosting.
  • Authorities, where the law requires it.

5. International transfers

Some providers store data outside Nigeria (for example in the EU or US). We only use providers with appropriate safeguards, as required by the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023.

6. Cookies and local storage

We use local storage only for essential features: bag, wishlist, currency and staying signed in. Clearing your browser data removes them.

7. How long we keep it

Order and payment records: up to 6 years for tax and accounting. Account data: until you delete your account. Waitlist and restock requests: until the alert is sent or you unsubscribe.

8. Your rights

Under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 — and, if you’re in the UK or EU, the GDPR — you can ask to access, correct, delete or receive a copy of your data, restrict or object to its use, and withdraw consent at any time. Message us and we’ll respond within 30 days. You can also complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC) or your local data protection authority.

9. Security

Passwords are encrypted by our sign-in provider, payments go through certified processors, and database access is restricted. If a breach affects you, we’ll tell you and the regulator as the law requires.

10. Children

T.GANG is for adults. We don’t knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

11. Changes

We’ll update the date at the top of this page when this policy changes.

See also our Return & Exchange Policy.
