Customer care

Returns &
exchanges.

Last updated 27 October 2026

We want you to love how it fits. If it isn’t right, here’s how returns and exchanges work at T.GANG.

1. Return window

You have 14 days from delivery to tell us you’d like to return or exchange an item.

2. What can be returned

  • Items must be unworn, unwashed and in their original condition and packaging, with all tags attached.
  • For hygiene reasons, bodysuits, swimwear and underwear are final sale unless faulty.
  • Sale items can be exchanged or returned for store credit (a gift card) only, not refunded to your card.
  • Gift cards are non-refundable and can’t be exchanged for cash.

3. How to start a return

  1. Message us on WhatsApp +234 915 999 9813 with your order reference (for example TG-…), the item and the reason.
  2. We’ll confirm it’s eligible and send you the return address and instructions.
  3. Pack the item securely and send it within 7 days of our confirmation. Keep your tracking number.

4. Refunds

Once your return arrives and passes inspection (usually within 3 working days), we refund the item price to your original payment method through Paystack or Flutterwave. Banks usually take 5–10 working days to show it. Orders paid by bank transfer or on WhatsApp are refunded to a Nigerian bank account you confirm. Original shipping, customs duties and taxes are not refundable unless the item was faulty or wrong.

5. Exchanges

Want a different size or colour? We’ll exchange it if stock allows. If it doesn’t, you can choose another item, store credit or a refund (sale items: store credit).

6. Damaged or wrong items

Message us within 48 hours of delivery with photos of the item and packaging. We’ll cover return shipping and send a replacement or give a full refund including shipping.

7. Return shipping

For change-of-mind returns and exchanges, you pay return shipping. Within Lagos and Nigeria any courier is fine; international returns must be sent with a tracked service such as DHL Express or FedEx, and you’re responsible for any return duties. If the mistake is ours, we pay.

8. TG Rewards

Points earned on returned items are removed. Points used on a refunded order are restored to your account.

9. Questions

Message us on WhatsApp +234 915 999 9813.

See also our Privacy Policy.
